 

 

**QUICK-BROKER**

 

**Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

 

Version: 1.0

Initial Market: Ishaka, Uganda

Primary Users: KIU students and rental property owners/landlords

 

\---

 

1\. Product Overview

 

Quick-Broker is a rental-discovery platform designed to connect students at KIU with landlords offering available accommodation in and around Ishaka.

 

The platform will make it easier for students to:

 

\- Discover available rentals

\- Search according to their budget

\- Compare different houses

\- Understand the actual costs of living in each house

\- See distance and estimated transport time from KIU

\- Understand facilities such as kitchens, bathrooms and toilets

\- Request and confirm house inspections

\- Contact landlords through phone or WhatsApp

\- Save preferred houses

\- Identify verified landlords

\- Know whether a listing is still available

 

For landlords, Quick-Broker will provide a way to:

 

\- Advertise available accommodation

\- Provide detailed information about their houses

\- Receive inspection requests

\- Communicate with prospective tenants

\- Confirm inspections

\- Update occupancy status

 

\---

 

2\. Product Goal

 

The main goal is to make finding student accommodation in Ishaka faster, clearer and more trustworthy.

 

Quick-Broker should reduce the common problem of students having to physically move around looking for houses without knowing:

 

\- Where the house is

\- How much it really costs

\- Whether it is still available

\- What facilities it has

\- How far it is from KIU

\- How they will access electricity and water

\- Whether the person advertising it is a genuine landlord

 

\---

 

3\. Target Users

 

3.1 Students

 

Primarily students at KIU looking for accommodation in Ishaka.

 

Students should be able to:

 

\- Create an account

\- Browse available listings

\- Search and filter houses

\- Save houses

\- Compare houses

\- Request inspections

\- Contact landlords

\- Confirm/complete inspections

\- Indicate whether they are interested

\- Report that they are not interested

\- Eventually indicate that they rented a house

 

3.2 Landlords

 

Landlords/property owners offering student accommodation.

 

Landlords should be able to:

 

\- Create an account

\- Verify their identity

\- Create rental listings

\- Add house information and photos

\- Receive inspection requests

\- Confirm inspections

\- Communicate with students

\- Confirm that a property is still available

\- Mark a property as occupied

Students

 

Students must create an account to access full rental information and interact with listings.

 

However, Quick-Broker should provide a limited preview before registration.

 

Before registration

 

A student can see:

 

\- Basic listing previews

\- Limited house information

\- General indication of available accommodation

 

After registration

 

A student can access:

 

\- Full listing information

\- Full photos

\- Detailed costs

\- Location/transport information

\- Save function

\- Compare function

\- Inspection requests

5\. Student Home/Search Experience

 

The main student screen should combine:

 

Search

 

Students can search for accommodation based on:

 

\- Area

\- Budget

\- Accommodation type

\- Distance from KIU

\- Transport time

\- House facilities

\- Electricity arrangement

\- Water arrangement

 

Available houses

 

Below the search area, students see available rental listings.

 

Listings should be presented clearly so students can quickly compare important information.

 

Example:

 

«Double Room — UGX 280,000/month

1.2 km from KIU

Approximately 8 minutes by boda

Kitchen: Present

Bathroom: Shared

Toilet: Shared

Electricity: Shared bill

Water: Community tap

✓ Identity-verified landlord»

6\. Rental Listing Information

 

Every listing should contain clear, standardized information.

 

Basic information

 

\- House type

\- Rent

\- General location/area

\- Photos

\- Availability status

 

Accommodation type

 

The initial accommodation categories are:

 

\- Single room

\- Double room

\- Self-contained single

\- Self-contained double

\- Other

 

Facilities

 

Kitchen

 

\- Present

\- Not present

 

Bathroom

 

\- Private

\- Shared

 

Toilet

 

\- Private

\- Shared

 

Flooring

 

\- Tiled

\- Cemented

 

Electricity

 

The landlord selects one:

 

\- Included in rent

\- Shared bill with neighbours

\- Personal electricity bill

 

Quick-Broker will not require landlords to estimate monthly electricity costs.

 

Water

 

The landlord selects one:

 

\- Included in rent

\- Shared payment with neighbours

\- Student pays separately

\- Community tap / water bought separately

 

Other charges

 

Landlords should be able to disclose additional charges that students may need to pay, such as security, garbage or other applicable charges.

 

\---

 

7\. Location & Transport

 

Location is one of Quick-Broker's major differentiating features.

 

Every listing should provide:

 

Distance from KIU

 

For example:

 

«1.4 km from KIU»

 

Estimated transport time

 

For example:

 

«Approximately 8 minutes by boda»

 

Where useful, walking time can also be displayed.

 

Area

 

Students should also be able to search by neighbourhood/area.

 

The purpose is not merely to tell students where the house is, but to help answer:

 

«“How convenient is this house for me as a KIU student?”»

 

\---

 

8\. Budget Filtering

 

Students should be able to specify:

 

Rent budget

 

Example:

 

«UGX 150,000–300,000/month»

 

Total monthly budget

 

This should take additional recurring charges into consideration where the information is available.

 

This prevents a student from selecting a house based only on advertised rent when the actual monthly cost may be substantially higher.

 

\---

 

9\. Save House

 

Students should be able to save interesting properties.

 

Each listing should have a:

 

«❤️ Save House»

 

option.

 

Saved houses should appear in:

 

«My Saved Houses»

 

Students can later remove saved properties.

 

\---

 

10\. Compare Houses

 

Students should be able to select multiple houses and compare them side-by-side.

 

The comparison should include important differences such as:

 

Information| House A| House B

Rent| UGX 250k| UGX 300k

Distance| 1.5 km| 800 m

Transport time| 10 min| 5 min

House type| Single| Double

Kitchen| Present| Present

Bathroom| Shared| Private

Toilet| Shared| Private

Electricity| Shared| Personal

Water| Community tap| Included

 

The goal is to allow students to make an informed choice without repeatedly opening different listings.

 

\---

 

11\. Landlord Verification

 

Quick-Broker should distinguish between ordinary accounts and identity-verified landlords.

 

Landlord identity verification will use:

 

\- National ID

\- Phone number

 

Students should not see the landlord's National ID number or ID document.

 

Instead, the listing can display:

 

«✓ Identity Verified»

 

Important distinction

 

Quick-Broker's identity verification does not mean Quick-Broker has verified ownership of the property.

 

The platform should not claim:

 

«“Property ownership verified”»

 

unless Quick-Broker actually has evidence to support that claim.

 

\---

 

12\. Property Listing Responsibility

 

Landlords are responsible for ensuring that:

 

\- Their listing information is accurate

\- The house actually exists

\- The house is available when advertised

\- Photos represent the property accurately

\- They have the authority to advertise the property

 

Quick-Broker provides the marketplace but does not automatically represent that it has verified property ownership.

 

Students should have a way to report inaccurate or suspicious listings.

 

\---

 

13\. Listing Availability

 

Each listing should have an availability status.

 

Examples:

 

\- Available

\- Inspection pending

\- Occupied

 

Landlords are responsible for updating the official occupancy status.

 

When a house is rented, the landlord can select:

 

«Mark as Occupied»

 

The listing should then stop appearing as an available rental.

 

\---

 

14\. Availability Confirmation

 

To reduce outdated listings, Quick-Broker should periodically ask landlords to confirm that their advertised houses are still available.

 

Example:

 

«Is this house still available?

 

✓ Yes, still available

✕ No, occupied»

 

This allows the platform to keep listings relatively current without requiring Quick-Broker staff to manually check every house.

 

\---

 

15\. Inspection System

 

Inspection is a core Quick-Broker feature.

 

A student can select:

 

«Request Inspection»

 

The student can provide a preferred date/time.

 

The landlord can:

 

\- Accept

\- Decline

\- Propose another suitable time

 

\---

 

16\. Inspection Verification

 

Quick-Broker will use a one-time inspection code.

 

Process

 

1\. Student requests inspection.

2\. Landlord accepts.

3\. Quick-Broker generates a one-time code.

4\. Student visits the property.

5\. Student shows the code to the landlord.

6\. Landlord confirms the code.

7\. The inspection becomes verified.

 

This provides stronger evidence that the student actually visited the property.

 

\---

 

17\. Contacting Landlords

 

Students should have three main actions:

 

📞 Call Landlord

 

Allows the student to make a normal phone call.

 

💬 WhatsApp Landlord

 

Allows direct WhatsApp communication.

 

📅 Request Inspection

 

Allows the student to initiate an inspection through Quick-Broker.

 

The platform should not force students to use only one communication method.

 

\---

 

18\. Post-Inspection Experience

 

After a verified inspection, the student should be able to indicate:

 

❤️ I'm interested

 

The student can continue communicating with the landlord.

 

❌ Not interested

 

The student can dismiss the property.

 

🏠 I've rented this house

 

The student can indicate that they eventually rented the property.

 

However, the landlord remains responsible for the official occupancy status of the listing.

 

\---

 

19\. Reviews

 

Reviews should be introduced as a trust feature.

 

Only students with a verified inspection should be eligible to review the property.

 

Reviews can cover areas such as:

 

\- Accuracy of listing

\- General experience

\- Water situation

\- Electricity arrangement

\- Location/transport

\- Other relevant observations

 

This prevents people who have never visited a property from freely reviewing it.

 

\---

 

20\. Monetisation Strategy

 

Quick-Broker should initially be completely free.

 

The initial objective is to build:

 

\- A strong student user base

\- A useful inventory of houses

\- A network of landlords

\- Trust

\- Regular usage

 

Monetisation should come later, after Quick-Broker has established a customer base.

 

Potential future monetisation options include:

 

Featured listings

 

Landlords can pay to give their property greater visibility.

 

Promotional listings

 

Landlords can pay for additional exposure.

 

Future transaction-related services

 

Quick-Broker may eventually introduce carefully designed fees around successful rentals or additional services.

 

Students should ideally continue to have free access to basic browsing and discovery.

23\. Trust & Safety Principles

 

Quick-Broker should be designed around the principle:

 

«“Verified person does not automatically mean verified property.”»

 

Important safeguards include:

 

\- National ID-based landlord identity verification

\- Phone verification

\- Clear verified-landlord badge

\- Reporting mechanism for suspicious listings

\- Landlord-controlled occupancy updates

\- Regular availability confirmation

\- One-time inspection codes

\- Reviews restricted to verified inspections

\- Clear distinction between platform verification and property ownership

 

\---

 

24\. Launch Strategy

 

The first version should remain focused.

 

Initial geographic focus

 

Ishaka, particularly accommodation relevant to KIU students.

 

Rather than trying to cover Uganda immediately, Quick-Broker should first establish a strong rental marketplace in this specific area.

 

Initial priority

 

The priority should be:

 

Good listings \+ accurate information \+ students \+ trust

 

rather than trying to add too many features at launch.

26\. Product Philosophy

 

Quick-Broker should not try to be simply another website where landlords post rooms.

 

Its core value should be:

 

«“Find the right student house without walking around Ishaka looking for one.”»

 

The platform should help a student answer the important questions before leaving their current location:

 

Can I afford it?

Where is it?

How far is it from KIU?

How do I get there?

What facilities does it have?

## 27. Architecture & Design Decisions (v1 Implementation)

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Status:** Implemented

This section documents all architectural and design decisions made during implementation, including rationale for each change from the original PRD.

---

### 27.1 User & Access Changes

| Decision | Original PRD | Implemented | Reason |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Landlord accounts** | Both students and landlords register | Students only — landlords are NOT users in v1 | Reduces complexity for MVP. Landlords are managed by admin; students contact landlords directly via phone/WhatsApp. This removes the need for landlord authentication, verification flows, and landlord dashboards. |
| **Auth method** | Phone OTP (implied) | Email OTP (ZeptoMail) | Email OTP is free — no SMS costs (~$1–4/month saved). ZeptoMail sandbox mode requires no domain verification, keeping costs at $0/month. Trade-off: some students may not check email regularly; WhatsApp OTP can be added in v1.1. |
| **User roles** | Student, Landlord | Student, Admin | Admin manages all listings on behalf of landlords. No landlord role needed in v1. |

---

### 27.2 Backend Architecture Changes

| Decision | Original PRD | Implemented | Reason |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Backend framework** | Not specified | Node.js + Express | Same language as frontend (React) — one developer can build everything. Lightweight, fast to build, direct PostgreSQL queries. |
| **Database** | Not specified | PostgreSQL (self-hosted on local device) | Full control, no vendor lock-in. Raw SQL queries. |
| **ORM** | Not specified | **None** — raw SQL with `pg` (node-postgres) | User requirement. No abstraction layer — full control over queries, no ORM learning curve, no dependency bloat. |
| **API style** | Not specified | REST (JSON) | Simple, well-understood, easy to test. Auto-generated from route definitions. |
| **Authentication** | Not specified | JWT (access + refresh tokens) | Stateless, scalable, standard. Access token (15 min) + refresh token (7 days). Stored in httpOnly cookies for security. |

---

### 27.3 Storage & Infrastructure Changes

| Decision | Original PRD | Implemented | Reason |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Image storage** | Not specified | Cloudflare R2 (S3-compatible) | User requirement. Free tier: 10GB storage, 10M requests/month. Zero egress fees — critical for a local server with limited bandwidth. Images delivered via Cloudflare CDN for fast access in Uganda. |
| **Hosting** | Not specified | Local device (Raspberry Pi / old laptop) | User requirement. Zero monthly cost. Full control. Low latency for Ishaka users. No vendor lock-in. |
| **Domain** | Not specified | DuckDNS (free subdomain) | Free dynamic DNS for changing home IP addresses. No custom domain cost (~$12/year saved). |
| **SSL** | Not specified | Let's Encrypt + certbot | Free HTTPS certificates with auto-renewal. No SSL cost. |
| **Dev environment** | Not specified | Docker Compose | One-command local development (`docker-compose up`). Same environment everywhere. Easy backup/reset. No version conflicts. |
| **Production process** | Not specified | PM2 | Auto-restart on crash/reboot. Simple process management. Zero cost. |
| **Cron jobs** | Not specified | node-cron | Periodic availability confirmation emails. Runs in-process, no external service needed. |

---

### 27.4 Frontend Changes

| Decision | Original PRD | Implemented | Reason |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Framework** | Not specified | React 18 + Vite + Tailwind CSS | Fast development, mobile-first responsive, PWA-capable. |
| **Mobile** | Not specified | PWA (Progressive Web App) | No app store friction. Works on any phone browser. Installable on Android/iOS. Zero app store fees. |
| **Navigation** | Not specified | Bottom tab bar (Home, Saved, Compare, Inspections) | Mobile-first thumb-friendly navigation. |
| **State management** | Not specified | React Context | Simple, no external dependency. Sufficient for auth state. |
| **Styling** | Not specified | Tailwind CSS | Rapid development, consistent design system, small bundle. |

### 27.6 Trade-offs & Risks

| Trade-off | Impact | Mitigation |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| No landlord accounts | Less self-service for landlords | Admin manages listings; landlords can call/WhatsApp to update |
| Email OTP (no SMS) | Some students may not check email | Add WhatsApp OTP in v1.1 (free, requires WhatsApp Business API approval) |
| Local hosting | Requires reliable internet + power | UPS for power backup; PM2 auto-restart; UptimeRobot alerts |
| No ORM | More manual SQL code | Full control; `pg` library is mature and stable |
| Home IP changes | DNS may be temporarily stale | DuckDNS auto-update client |
